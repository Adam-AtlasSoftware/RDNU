# Arm Neural Super Sampling v1.0.1 — repacked INT8 weights

`nss_w8.bin`, `nss.rdnm` and `nss.manifest.json` are generated by `runtime/tools/nss_export.py`
from Arm's `nss_v1_0_1_high_int8.pt` (https://huggingface.co/Arm/neural-super-sampling).

Modified by RDNU: the quantisation-aware-trained weights were re-quantised to int8 exactly as
the QAT graph does, the int32 bias and requantisation multipliers were precomputed, the
sigmoid output stages were tabulated, and the tensors were re-laid out for DP4a and wave-matrix
kernels. The network function is unchanged.
